About Me

Engineer, researcher and interdisciplinary thinker

I am an engineer and researcher with a background in signal processing and artificial intelligence. My work bridges the gap between biological computation and practical engineering, exploring how principles derived from neuroscience can inform the design of more efficient and capable AI systems.

My current research focuses on Spiking Neural Networks as a computational framework for audio signal processing, investigating how event-driven, sparse computation can address challenges in audio representation and compression.
