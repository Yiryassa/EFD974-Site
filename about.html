Professional Profile

Who I am

I am an engineer and researcher working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, digital signal processing and neural computation. My work is driven by a genuine curiosity about how biological systems process information so efficiently, and how these principles can be applied to build better AI systems.

My research focuses on Spiking Neural Networks — a class of neural network models that more closely resemble the dynamics of biological neurons — and their application to audio signal processing and compression. This brings together two fields I find deeply compelling: the mathematical richness of signal processing and the frontier challenges of modern AI.

I approach research with engineering rigour: I care about whether ideas work in practice, how they scale, and what real problems they can solve.

Research Philosophy

I believe that the most interesting progress happens at disciplinary boundaries. By bringing together ideas from neuroscience, signal processing and machine learning, I aim to contribute research that is both scientifically meaningful and practically grounded.
